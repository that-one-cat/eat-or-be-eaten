# scripts/world - map, seabed, water look, cameras, spatial cells

Presentation of the world: terrain, depth fog + caustics + refraction stack, per-size-band camera near/far, one MultiMesh per spatial cell (32-64 m). Playable ocean stays within +-4 km of the origin. Water look must degrade gracefully on Compatibility (no volumetric fog, no compute shaders).

## NPC crowd presentation (S4)

- `crowd_assets.gd` (`CrowdAssets`): species GLBs, palette colours, crowd meshes (blend shapes baked at mid growth for MultiMesh) and the `ambient` / `interactive` crowd material duplicates. `RIG_MODELS`: the giant shark node view is the rigged GLB with `m_boss_rig` (S7).
- `ambient_renderer.gd` (`AmbientRenderer`): ambient tier. One MultiMeshInstance3D per school (default; `-- --ambient-mode=chunk` = one per 64 m chunk per species, kept for timing), school wobble in `fish_swim.gdshader`, promoted / eaten members hidden by swap-last + `visible_instance_count`, respawns grow in; plankton swarm clusters around the camera from the `PlanktonField` density (glow pulse: `fish_swim.gdshader` `glow_pulse_*` / `glow_body`, set in `m_plankton_swim.tres` and `m_krill_abyss_swim.tres`); krill clouds (`SimWorld.krill`): one MultiMesh per field, one instance per alive krill (`KrillField` layout, 0.15-0.28 m, 4 m cells, radius 4 / 3 cells high / low and 2 cells vertically, low tier half the krill per cell), moved by the shader `krill` uniforms, an eaten krill hidden at once by swap-last, the cell loop clipped to the field grid; `bench_stats` `krill_instances`, `plankton_multimeshes` counts every swarm.
- `npc_views.gd` (`NpcViews`): interactive tier as one MultiMesh per species (bulk `MultiMesh.buffer` writes per frame), large actors and puffers as one GLB node each, food bits; `take_view` / `release_view` hand pooled ghost models to `FeedbackFx` (kill effects capped at 8).
- Quality tier: `scripts/ui/quality_settings.gd` (`-- --quality=low|high` or `user://settings.cfg [graphics] quality_tier`, else a renderer/GPU check; `QualitySettings.profile(tier)` = per-tier switches).

## Underwater look (S5)

- `depth_environment.gd` (`DepthEnvironment`): water colour, fog, ambient, sun scatter, glow by camera depth (turquoise 0 m -> teal 28 m -> navy 80 m); `apply_quality(tier)` sets glow levels, fog density multiplier and (high + Forward+) volumetric fog.
- `underwater_fx.gd` (`UnderwaterFx`): surface seen from below, light-shaft cones on a world grid around the camera, marine snow, refraction quad (high). `setup(camera, tier, depth_env)`; `bench_stats()`.
- Grey-box terrain uses `shaders/m_greybox_terrain.tres` (baked vertex light + caustics); env pieces use `shaders/m_env*.tres` (see `shaders/README.md`).
- Bench API: `bench_stats()` on `AmbientRenderer` and `NpcViews`, `presentation_stats()` on the reef root (`reef_greybox.gd`).

## Map dressing (S5)
- `reef.tscn` (`reef.gd`, extends `reef_greybox.gd`) = the grey-box reef + `MapDressing` + the depth-coloured seabed (`GreyboxTerrain`: `build_obstacles` / `build_edge_walls` off, `apron_m` 120, `depth_colors`, `steep_color`, `facet_jitter`). `main.tscn` runs it from the main menu (`run_scene`, S6 flow); `reef_greybox.tscn` stays for the swim tour, tests and as a fallback.
- `MapDressing.compute_placements(dressing, map, seabed, piece_index) -> Array` is static, node-free and deterministic; `build()` turns it into one MultiMeshInstance3D per piece variant per 64 m chunk (group `env_dressing`, no shadows, `visibility_range_end` = rule `view_m` x tier + chunk radius, kelp with `m_env_kelp.tres`) and one MeshInstance3D per set piece. No physics bodies. Low tier draws `dressing_density_mult` (0.55) of the env_small scatter (never kelp).
- Food sites (food task): `food_placement.gd` (`FoodPlacement`, static, node-free `derive(dressing, map, seabed_with_proxies, piece_index)` -> the baked food site records; run by `tools/dressing/bake_food.gd`, writes `maps/reef_food.json`); `MapDressing.build(..., food_sites)` draws them (eggs / snails in the scatter MultiMeshes, edible decor = its decor instance, both exempt from the low-tier thinning) and `set_site_visible()`; `food_site_view.gd` (`FoodSiteView`) hides eaten sites and shows respawned ones from the events.
- Dev args of `reef.tscn`: `--show-obstacles` (translucent sim proxies), `--views=spawn,kelp,...` + `--hold=<frames>` (fixed review cameras, see `reef.gd` VIEWS).
- Layout of set pieces, obstacle proxies and cliff segments: `tools/dressing/reef_layout.gd` (data format: `docs/data/dressing.md`; invariants and re-bake order: `docs/systems/dressing-collision.md`).
- `dressing_proxies.gd` (`DressingProxies`, fix-rock-collision): static, node-free `derive(dressing, map, piece_index)` -> one `obb` collision proxy per solid placement (dressing `solid_pieces`), `obb()` / `record()` (also used for the a20 clump proxies), `proxies_text()`, `build_index()` (text of `data/env/pieces.json` incl. per-variant `bounds`). Run by `tools/dressing/bake_proxies.gd` (writes `maps/reef_proxies.json`) and `reef_layout.gd --index`; the sim never calls it. `--show-obstacles` also draws the baked proxies.

## God overlays (T1.2, dev builds only)
- `DevOverlays` (`dev_overlays.gd`, Node3D, top-level child of the map root created lazily by `GodContext.overlays()` on the first `overlay` toggle; read-only, rule 1): `set_enabled(name, on)` for `obstacles | zones | grid | detect | bitecue`, `is_enabled`, `vertex_count`, `names()`. Line meshes, unshaded, no shadows, depth test off and high render priority (works in Compatibility). `zones` = wire box per `map.zones` (built once); `grid` = SpatialGrid lattice within 5 cells of the player (rebuilt when the player changes cell); `detect` = horizontal + vertical rings of `detection_radius_m` (red), `flee_radius_m` (yellow) and `behaviour.boost_within_m` (orange) for the nearest 24 NPCs within 60 m (rebuilt every 4 frames). `bitecue` (T1.11, an A/B experiment, not a feature; default off, never persisted) = ONE pooled billboard ring `MeshInstance3D` (`BiteCue`, unshaded, alpha 0.4 constant, no shadows, no depth test) around the creature `SimWorld.peek_bite_target(player)` names, sized to its collision radius, hidden without a target, moved every frame; the node exists only while on (`off` frees it). Capture: `tools/capture/bitecue_ab.godscript`. Limits: no per-frame cost while off; `grid` / `detect` rebuild meshes in GDScript.
- `obstacles` delegates to the map root's `Reef.set_obstacles_visible(on)` (reef.gd; builds the magenta `Obstacles` mesh on the first `on` through the same `_show_obstacles` as `--show-obstacles`, then only flips `visible`). The grey-box reef (`reef_greybox.tscn`) has no such method and always draws its obstacles, so there `overlay obstacles` answers "not available".
- `GameLoop._process` multiplies the frame delta by `world.debug.time_scale` when `world.debug != null` (God `speed`); `Engine.time_scale` stays FeedbackFx's.

## Abilities and boss FX (S6)
- `SonarFx` (`sonar_fx.gd`): on the player's `SONAR_PULSE` an expanding shell + horizontal ring (`fx_sonar_wave.gdshader`, additive) and one camera-facing ring per contact for the pulse duration (`fx_sonar_contact.gdshader`, one MultiMesh, seen through walls). Tint + shape by `rel`: smaller = blue thin ring, similar = pale ring + dot, bigger = orange thick ring with ticks (colour-blind safe: shape repeats the colour).
- `BossFx` (`boss_fx.gd`): `BOSS_TELL` wind-up = nose-up arch + tremble + squash, warm rim/glow flash on a per-node duplicate of the boss swim material, edge vignette (`Hud.vignette`) when the player is the target; `BOSS_DASH` = velocity-aligned bubble streak trail + near-miss shake; `STUNNED` = roll wobble + cool rim pulse. Pose tweaks run after `NpcViews` (`process_priority` 20, `NpcViews.view_node(id)`). Creates `BossView`.
- `BossView` (`boss_view.gd`, S7): drives the boss `FishRig` from state and events: swim rate from speed, turn bend from yaw rate, `BOSS_TELL` gape + coil pose (on top of the BossFx nose-up arch), `BOSS_DASH` burst stroke, bite snap on the boss's bites.
- `FeedbackFx` handles `BOSS_DEFEATED` (finisher: slow-mo, full punch, shake, warm screen flash, giant "CHOMP!!", bursts); the boss `DIED` of the same tick gives the usual X-ray flash + skeleton; giant remains are `NpcViews` food bits. S7: the killing bite also pops a (bigger) "CHOMP!" from the victim's `DIED` event (the NPC is removed in that tick); a tree pause ends a running slow-mo.
- `DemoFx` (`--demo-fx`) auto-picks the first offered mutation at the band-up (the pick pause stays on). `--demo-fx` and `--autopilot` only work in debug builds (editor binary, debug exports); a release export ignores them.
- `S6Demo` (`s6_demo.gd`, capture-only `-- --s6-demo=hud|pick|sonar|presence|boss`): fixture for the S6 review captures through `SimDebug` / `Progression.grant`; never active in normal play.
